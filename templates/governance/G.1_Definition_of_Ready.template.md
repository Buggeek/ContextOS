## Purpose
Define what must be true before work enters a sprint or is accepted for implementation.

---

## DoR Rules (Non-Negotiable)

A work item is “Ready” only if:

1. Objective is clearly stated.
2. Scope is explicit.
3. Acceptance Criteria are testable.
4. Dependencies are declared (services, integrations, permissions, data).
5. Impacted systems are identified (A.1 references).
6. Risks/unknowns are documented.
7. Required design/assets exist (if applicable).
8. Owner is assigned.
9. Validation plan exists (manual/automated).

---

## Required References
A “Ready” item must link to:

- Epic
- System Map (A.1)
- Relevant Entities (A.4)
- Relevant Integrations (A.5) if applicable
