# S.1 Vision
Version: 0.1.0  
Owner: Founder  

---

## Purpose

Build a focused digital tool that solves a specific problem
clearly and reliably.

---

## Vision Statement

Create a simple, fast, and trustworthy product
that users enjoy using daily.

---

## Strategic Narrative

The product is built and evolved by a single founder.
Speed matters, but coherence matters more.

The product must remain understandable to:
- the founder
- future collaborators
- AI systems assisting development

---

## Target Audience

- Primary users: early adopters
- Secondary users: future collaborators (devs, designers)

---

## Time Horizon

- 6 months: product-market fit validation
- 12 months: stable user base
- 24 months: optional team expansion

---

## Success Definition

- Users return regularly
- Features remain coherent
- Development remains fast
- Context is not lost over time

---

## Constraints

- Limited time
- Limited resources
- Solo execution

---

## Dependencies

- Hosting provider
- Auth provider
- Payment provider (future)
