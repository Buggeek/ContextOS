# Sample Mid-Size Organization
## Chain of Restaurants with Digital Channel

Compliance profile: `strict`

This example demonstrates how Context OS applies to a non-software-native organization.

The organization is a mid-size restaurant chain with:
- Physical locations
- Central operations
- Digital ordering (web + mobile)
- Delivery integrations
- Inventory and supply workflows

The goal is to show that Context OS scales beyond startups and pure software teams.

This example includes only the Minimum Operational Map (MOM).
No automation. No agents. No SaaS.

---

## Organizational Context

- 25–50 restaurant locations
- Central operations team
- Digital ordering channel
- Third-party delivery integrations
- ERP / POS systems
- High operational dependency on human know-how

---

## Why Context OS Here

Typical problems in this type of organization:

- Operational knowledge lives in people, not systems
- Digital and physical channels drift apart
- Changes in menu or pricing break downstream systems
- Dependencies between POS, inventory, and delivery are implicit
- AI initiatives amplify inconsistency instead of reducing it

Context OS introduces structural visibility without disrupting operations.

---

## Scope of This Example

Included:
- Vision
- Product Map
- System Map
- Core Data Entities
- Governance (DoR / DoD)

Excluded:
- Detailed workflows
- Automation
- Tool integrations
- Domain-level MOMs (future phase)

This is the corporate-level MOM.
