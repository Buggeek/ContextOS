# S.1 Vision
## Version: 0.1.0
Last Updated: 2026-02-19  
Owner: Executive Team  

---

## Purpose

Deliver a consistent, efficient, and scalable restaurant experience
across physical and digital channels.

---

## Scope

In scope:
- Corporate-level omnichannel operations
- Menu, pricing, ordering, payment, inventory, fulfillment, and customer communication

Out of scope:
- HR and hiring operations
- Store-level staffing and scheduling optimization (future)

---

## Vision Statement

To operate a restaurant network where customer experience,
operational efficiency, and digital channels evolve coherently.

---

## Strategic Narrative

The organization is transitioning from a location-centric operation
to a unified omnichannel model.

Growth depends on:
- Operational consistency
- Reliable digital ordering
- Inventory and pricing accuracy
- Fast adaptation without operational chaos

---

## Target Audience

- End customers
- Restaurant managers
- Operations team
- Digital product team

---

## Time Horizon

- 12 months: stable omnichannel operation
- 24 months: scalable digital growth
- 5 years: data-driven operational intelligence

---

## Success Definition

- Reduced order errors
- Faster menu and pricing changes
- Consistent experience across channels
- Reduced operational friction

---

## Success Metrics

- Order error rate (in-store vs digital)
- Time-to-propagate menu/pricing changes across all channels
- Inventory accuracy per location
- Support/incident volume related to ordering and fulfillment

---

## Constraints

- Regulatory (food safety, payments)
- Legacy POS systems
- Physical operation constraints

---

## Dependencies

- POS providers
- Delivery platforms
- ERP / inventory systems
