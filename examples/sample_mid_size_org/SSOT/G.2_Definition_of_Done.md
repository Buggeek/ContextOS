# G.2 Definition of Done (DoD)
## Version: 0.1.0
Last Updated: 2026-02-19  
Owner: Product & Tech  

---

## Purpose

Define what must be true before work is considered complete and safe to release.

## DoD Rules (Non-Negotiable)

A change is Done only if:

1. Works across all channels in scope.
2. POS and digital systems are aligned (no silent divergence).
3. No inventory inconsistencies are introduced (or mitigations are documented).
4. Documentation/context artifacts are updated as needed.
5. Operations are notified when operational workflows are impacted.
6. A rollback plan exists for high-risk changes.

---

## Exceptions

Exceptions require explicit approval by Product & Tech leadership.

---

## Enforcement

- Enforced during review before rollout.
- High-risk changes require documented rollback evidence.
