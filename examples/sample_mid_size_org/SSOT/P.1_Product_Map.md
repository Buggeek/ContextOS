# P.1 Product Map
Version: 0.1.0  
Owner: Product & Operations  

---

## Product Surfaces

- Physical restaurant experience
- Web ordering
- Mobile ordering
- Delivery partner channels
- Admin / operations tools

---

## Core User Journeys

1. Customer places an order (in-store or online)
2. Restaurant fulfills the order
3. Inventory is updated
4. Payment is processed
5. Order status is communicated

---

- Pricing and promotions
- Order intake and routing
- Inventory tracking
- Customer communication

---

## Primary User Roles

- Customer
- Restaurant staff
- Store manager
- Operations admin
