# G.1 Definition of Ready (DoR)
## Version: 0.1.0
Last Updated: 2026-02-19  
Owner: Product & Tech  

---

## Purpose

Define what must be true before work is accepted for implementation.

## DoR Rules (Non-Negotiable)

A change is Ready only if:

1. Objective is clear.
2. Scope is explicit.
3. Impacted restaurants/locations are identified.
4. Impacted systems are listed (A.1 references).
5. Data entities affected are declared (A.4 references).
6. Rollout risk is documented (including rollback expectations).
7. Operations are informed when operational workflows are impacted.

## Exceptions

Exceptions are allowed only with Ops approval.

---

## Enforcement

- Enforced as a checklist during planning/review.
- Items failing DoR are blocked from execution.
