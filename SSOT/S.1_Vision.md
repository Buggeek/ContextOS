# S.1 Vision
## Version: 0.1.0
Last Updated: 2026-08-20
Owner: Context OS Maintainers  

---

## Purpose

Build and evolve Context OS as an inspectable, governable Organizational
Context Runtime that enables AI-native organizations to govern their own
evolution.

---

## Scope

In scope:
- Conceptual foundations and principles
- Architecture + operational model (MOM, governance, agent operating model)
- Document taxonomy + templates
- Examples that demonstrate adoption paths
- Runtime contracts and local runtime tooling
- Validator, CLI, and Context Readiness capabilities

Out of scope:
- Shipping a SaaS product in this repository
- Enterprise integrations/connectors as a default requirement

---

## Vision Statement

Context OS becomes a clear, minimal, and auditable runtime for context, memory,
governance, and activation so humans and agents can execute with alignment.

AI-native means humans, agents, systems, skills, tools, and workflows can
participate in one context-, evidence-, memory-, and authority-bound
organizational system. It does not mean agents replace human accountability.

---

## Strategic Narrative

This repository treats **context as infrastructure**, not as documentation.

The project succeeds when:
- adopters can assess readiness and implement a coherent MOM quickly,
- governance is evidence-based,
- and the system remains minimal while scaling to complex organizations.

---

## Target Audience

- Primary: founders, organizational leaders, product and operating owners, and
  human-agent system builders
- Secondary: teams across Strategy, Product, Technology, Marketing, Sales,
  Customer Success, Finance, Legal, People, Operations, Research, and Data

---

## Time Horizon

- Current: establish Organizational Memory and decision continuity in v0.8
- Next: prove governed Contextual Reasoning in v0.9
- v1.0: demonstrate the complete Organizational Context Runtime evolution loop

---

## Success Definition

- Implementations are coherent and reproducible.
- Readiness assessments are actionable and reproducible.
- Changes to the framework are reviewable and auditable.
- The MOM stays minimal while remaining operationally useful.
- Organizational memory preserves decisions, evidence, learning, temporal
  state, and supersession without becoming a second SSOT.
- Increased agent participation does not weaken human authority or evidence
  boundaries.

---

## Success Metrics

- Time-to-first-MOM (adopters)
- % of templates/examples that conform to declared compliance profile
- Broken-link count in framework docs (should trend to zero)
- Number of coherent external implementations (quality > stars)
- % of retrieved memory with complete provenance and authority lineage
- % of completed Missions with explicit evidence, learning, and re-anchoring

---

## Constraints

- Minimalism over feature sprawl
- Governance over autonomy
- Evidence over trust
- Repository-first, tool-optional
