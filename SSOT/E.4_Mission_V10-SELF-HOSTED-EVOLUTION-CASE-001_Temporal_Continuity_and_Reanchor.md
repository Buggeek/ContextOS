## Decision

Accept the integrated benchmark implementation as the governed change produced
by the first v1.0 self-hosted case. Re-anchor current v1.0 work on its committed
runtime state while preserving the prior Context Version as historically valid
but no longer current.

Do not claim complete historical resolution for the prior version. Its Mission
Packet source was uncommitted at capture, so later source change can be verified
only partially. The immutable identity remains valid and the continuity gap is
explicit.

---

## Learning

Explicit capture is stronger than retrospective reconstruction, but a
content-free Context Version cannot later resolve a changed source that was
never committed or retained by its source system. The correct result is
partial historical verification, not reconstruction.

The complete evolution loop does not require every Mission to perform every
product stage. It requires traceable continuity across the stages that
legitimately apply and exact evidence for released governed-change paths.
