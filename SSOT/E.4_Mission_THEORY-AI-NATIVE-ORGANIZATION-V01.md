## Canonical Organizational Model

The proposed six-plane model was accepted with two refinements:

1. The planes are concurrent organizational control surfaces, not a sequential
   stack.
2. Context Core becomes Context and Memory Core so continuity, temporal state,
   retention, and supersession are explicit rather than implied.

The canonical planes are:

1. Direction
2. Context and Memory Core
3. Sense and Activate
4. Organize and Execute
5. Measure and Learn
6. Govern and Evolve

The canonical organizational cognition mapping makes perception, attention,
working memory, long-term memory, interpretation, reasoning, action, feedback,
learning, and controlled plasticity organizational functions of the complete
governed loop. No agent is treated as the organization's brain.

## Truth and Lifecycle Decision

A single linear truth lifecycle would collapse distinct semantics. The Theory
therefore preserves three axes:

| Axis | States |
|---|---|
| Epistemic support | observed, declared, inferred, derived, unknown |
| Governance lifecycle | suggested, draft, reviewed, approved, canonical |
| Strategic belief | hypothesis, verified, deprecated |

This permits observed evidence to remain non-canonical, approved drafts to
remain unverified, canonical artifacts to contain hypotheses, and unknowns to
remain explicit.

## GraphRAG Decision

GraphRAG is an optional retrieval and reasoning strategy over the derived
Context Graph and related evidence.

It may support multi-hop selection, relationship-aware retrieval, impact
analysis, subgraph retrieval, context planning, and explainable selection.

It is not Context OS, the SSOT, the authoritative graph, an ontology, or a
replacement for provenance, governance, validation, or Context Activation.

No GraphRAG implementation was authorized or performed.

## Claims Decision

The Theory uses:

```text
supported | partially supported | not yet tested | contradicted | superseded
```

Current self-hosting supports progressive self-governance inside this
repository. Package sufficiency, bounded retrieval, Mission learning,
authority under growing autonomy, and manual-context reduction are only
partially supported. Cross-domain universality, learning-improved Activation,
and pattern consolidation remain not yet tested.

No architectural possibility was recorded as proven product truth.

## Learning

- AI-native is an organizational governance property, not a count of agents or
  AI tools.
- The six-plane model becomes simpler when treated as concurrent control
  surfaces around one evolution loop.
- Epistemic support, governance lifecycle, and strategic belief must remain
  orthogonal to prevent evidence from silently becoming truth.
- Organizational Memory is continuity with authority and time, not storage.
- GraphRAG should earn its place through retrieval evidence rather than enter
  v0.8 as an architecture-first dependency.
- Theory becomes useful when its claims can be downgraded, contradicted, or
  superseded by operational evidence.
